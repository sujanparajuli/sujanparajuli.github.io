Emergency Department Cost Prediction

Healthcare ML · HCUP NEDS 2016 · Mentor: Dr. Natalie Cartwright

Analyzed 10,000,000 HCUP NEDS 2016 records using demographic, insurance, location, income, and diagnosis variables. Built an XGBoost workflow with 10,000-row data chunks, filtering, and categorical encoding; an 80/20 training/test split yielded a test R-squared of 0.28 and identified limitations in diagnosis grouping. Restricted patient-level data remains private.
