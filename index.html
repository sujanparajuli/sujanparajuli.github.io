Vertex Magic Trees

Undergraduate combinatorics research with Prof. Dan McQuillan studying vertex-magic total labelings of trees through proof, computation, and extremal arguments.

  • Developed sum identities and averaging arguments connecting vertex labels, edge labels, leaves, and the magic constant.
  • Investigated boundedness results and tested difficult parameter regimes computationally.
  • Constructed a vertex-magic example with n = 5 and ℓ = 8 for a ratio below √3.
  • Presented the work through a research poster and technical report.
